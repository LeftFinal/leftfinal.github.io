Polar Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Polar is an end-to-end encrypted chat app for communities, direct messages and voice calls. It is run by LeftFinal ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Polar apps for Android, iOS and Windows and the Polar server we run, which they connect to by default. The Polar server can also be run by others; if you connect to a server someone else runs, that operator holds the data described here and their own privacy policy applies.

The short version: we cannot read your messages, your profile or your communities' names and channels. They are encrypted on your device before they leave it, and only the people you talk to hold the keys. The server keeps only what it needs to deliver messages and enforce permissions, and for no longer than it needs to.

2. Other data we handle

Account and sign-in

Your password never reaches us. Sign-in uses the OPAQUE protocol, so the server stores only a record it can check a sign-in against, not the password or anything it could be recovered from. We store your devices' public keys and a session for each signed-in device, with the day it was last active.

Attachments and backups

Files you send are encrypted on your device before upload, so we store only ciphertext. An upload that is never sent is deleted after 24 hours. A sent file is kept until the devices it is owed to have fetched it, and at most 30 days. If you turn on encrypted backup, we keep your last three backup versions; we cannot read them. A community can choose to share encrypted message history with new members; that history is kept as the community's settings say and deleted when sharing is turned off or the channel is deleted.

Presence and typing

Your online status is shared with people you share a community or direct message with, unless you turn it off. It is held in memory only and expires within 90 seconds. Typing indicators are encrypted and never stored.

Notifications

To send notifications we store your device's push token, encrypted at rest. Notifications carry no names, IDs or message content in the clear; they contain only an encrypted payload. Tokens the push service reports as invalid are deleted.

Reports

If you report a message, the app sends us that message's text, and any surrounding messages you choose to include, so our safety team can review it. The app tells you this before you send. The text revealed in a report is deleted 180 days after the report is resolved, and resolved or closed reports are deleted after a year. A report may be kept longer under a legal hold, for example where the law requires us to preserve evidence of child sexual abuse material.

Server logs

Logs contain no user IDs, usernames or IP addresses and are kept for 7 days. Metrics contain no identifiers.

3. Permissions the Android app asks for

  • Microphone: for voice calls and voice notes, only while you use them.
  • Notifications: to tell you about new messages and incoming calls.
  • Network access: to connect to the Polar server.
  • Battery optimisation exemption (optional): so notifications arrive promptly.

Photos you take from the message box are taken with your phone's own camera app and are not saved to your gallery; Polar does not hold the camera permission. Files you attach are picked with the system file picker. Polar contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Who else receives data

  • Our hosting provider, which runs the server and stores the data above. The server is hosted in South Africa.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) on Android and Apple (APNs) on iOS, to deliver notifications. They see your device's push token, the timing and an encrypted payload. On Android without Google services you can use UnifiedPush, and the distributor you choose sees the same.
  • Klipy, if you search for GIFs. Our server makes the search on your behalf, so Klipy sees your search words but not your IP address or account.
  • Our email provider, if you add an email address, to send confirmation and reset emails.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else unless the law requires it. If we receive a lawful demand, we can disclose only what is in the table above; we do not have your message content.

5. Your rights

  • Access: you can export everything the server holds about you as a file from Settings in the app. Your messages are on your devices and can be exported there.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account in Settings. It is deleted 30 days later; until then you can cancel. Deleting removes your devices, sessions, sent messages still waiting for delivery, files, backups and sign-in record. Communities you own are deleted unless you hand them to someone else first. Records needed to keep shared community history consistent are kept with your identity replaced by a "deleted user" marker, and reports involving you are kept with your ID and any note you wrote removed.
  • Correction and objection: you can change your username, profile and email in the app at any time, or contact us.

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for and, for abuse prevention, in our legitimate interest in keeping Polar safe. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under the GDPR or South Africa's POPIA, including the right to complain to your data protection authority (in South Africa, the Information Regulator).

6. Security

Messages and calls use end-to-end encryption (MLS for messages, encrypted media frames for calls), connections use TLS, and data that the server must hold is encrypted at rest where possible. No system is perfectly secure, but we design Polar so that a breach of our servers would not expose your messages.

7. Children

You must be at least 13 to use Polar, or 16 where the law requires it. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child is using Polar, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
